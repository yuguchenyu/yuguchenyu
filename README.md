## About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science and Technology at **Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)**. My research focuses on 3D computer vision, generative models, and data-efficient learning.

## Research

### Long-Tailed 3D Point Cloud Dataset Distillation

**Jiahao You\*, Xu Han\*, Jinfeng Xu, Xianzhi Li**  
AAAI 2027 submission | [arXiv:2607.26763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26763)

- Developed an adaptive synthetic-budget allocation strategy, global-local feature alignment, and class-prior-aware supervision for long-tailed point-cloud dataset distillation.
- Improved classification accuracy on ShapeNet55 by **7.0 percentage points** over the previous state of the art under a limited synthetic-data budget.
- Evaluated the approach across point-cloud classification, point-cloud segmentation, and long-tailed image datasets.
- Contributed to problem formulation, method design, implementation, experiments, visualization, and manuscript writing.

### Physically Plausible Simulation Environments from a Single RGB Image

- Investigating scene generation from extremely sparse visual observations for low-cost VLA evaluation and a reduced sim-to-real gap.
- Designing a scene-first pipeline that preserves global geometry and object relationships before instance segmentation and physical-property modeling.

## Selected Projects

### Fault-Tolerant Multimodal Foundation Models for Spaceborne Computing

- Designing reliable inference and fault-tolerance mechanisms for multimodal remote-sensing models deployed on resource-constrained satellite hardware.
- Building bit-level fault injection for LoRA modules, MoE routers and experts, intermediate activations, and KV caches.
- Studying silent data corruption caused by single-event upsets and developing lightweight validators, adaptive checkpoint rollback, and selective re-inference.

### Intelligent Operations Platform for Kylin OS

**Grand Prize, China College Students' Challenge Cup Competition**

- Built a monitoring, anomaly-detection, and root-cause-analysis platform using Prometheus, Grafana, and hierarchical agents.
- Processed CPU, memory, GPU, disk I/O, and network metrics and extracted time-series features.
- Trained streaming decision trees, online random forests, and LSTM models for failure detection, severity assessment, and node-level risk reporting.
- Integrated structured risk reports with large language models to produce root-cause analyses and operations recommendations.

### Huawei Intelligent Base "Light Cone" Project

- Developed lightweight mobile facial effects and GIF-generation features.
- Implemented facial-effect modules, integrated frontend and backend interfaces, and supported mobile adaptation and debugging.
